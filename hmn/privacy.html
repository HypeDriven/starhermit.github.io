1. Scope thiab peb yog leej twg

StarHermit yog ua haujlwm los ntawm Hype Driven Development Inc., lub koom haum British Columbia ("StarHermit," "peb," "peb," lossis "peb"). Txoj cai no siv rau starhermit.com thiab cov vev xaib, cov ntawv thov, APIs, hosting, kev faib khoom ua si, kev lag luam, zej zog, thiab cov kev pabcuam txhawb nqa uas txuas rau nws (sib sau ua ke, "Platform").

Txoj cai no tsis tswj hwm tus tshaj tawm tus kheej cov ntaub ntawv kev coj ua hauv kev ua si lossis ntawm kev pabcuam sab nraud. Cov kev coj ua no raug them los ntawm tus tshaj tawm tsab ntawv ceeb toom ntiag tug.

2. Cov ntaub ntawv peb sau

Account thiab profile ntaub ntawv

Thaum koj kos npe nrog Google lossis txuas rau lwm tus account xws li GitHub, peb tau txais cov ntaub ntawv tus kws kho mob tsim muaj, xws li koj lub npe, email chaw nyob, duab profile, username, thiab tus neeg muab kev pabcuam tus lej cim. Peb tsis tau txais koj tus password Google lossis GitHub password. Peb kuj sau cov ntsiab lus profile, nyiam, thiab cov chaw koj xaiv ntxiv.

Platform thiab kev ua si

Peb sau cov ntaub ntawv xav tau los ua haujlwm rau koj lub tsev qiv ntawv thiab lub Platform, suav nrog cov kev ua si tau saib, tau txais, ntsia, tso tawm, lossis xav tau; sijhawm ua si; huab txuag; kev ua tiav; cov thawj coj; kev sib tham sib ntau; phooj ywg thiab nyob; kev tshuaj xyuas, kev ntsuam xyuas, kev tshaj tawm, cov lus, thiab lwm yam ntsiab lus; thiab cov txheej xwm kev xa tawm los ntawm kev ua si los ntawm StarHermit cov kev pabcuam.

Cov ntaub ntawv tsim tawm thiab tshaj tawm

Yog tias koj xa lossis tswj ib qho kev ua si, peb sau cov ntaub ntawv txuas thiab cov metadata, tsim, tso tawm thiab cov ntaub ntawv ua tswv cuab, pab neeg tso cai, cov ntaub ntawv khaws cia, API siv, thiab kev sib txuas lus. Rau kev ua kom tau nyiaj lossis ua pov thawj, peb tseem tuaj yeem sau cov ntaub ntawv raug cai, kev lag luam, se, kev nplua, kev them nyiaj, thiab cov ntaub ntawv them nyiaj.

Kev lag luam

Thaum koj ua ib qho kev yuav khoom lossis tau txais cov nyiaj them poob haujlwm, peb sau cov ntaub ntawv sib pauv xws li cov khoom, nyiaj, txiaj, sijhawm, xwm txheej, tau txais nyiaj, rov qab, thiab hom kev them nyiaj. Daim npav thiab cov ntaub ntawv hauv tuam txhab nyiaj raug tswj hwm los ntawm peb cov chaw them nyiaj es tsis yog khaws cia ncaj qha los ntawm StarHermit. Rau kev lag luam cov cuab yeej cuab tam digital, peb tuaj yeem ua cov chaw nyob hauv hnab nyiaj thiab cov cim kev lag luam pej xeem.

Cov khoom siv, kev siv, thiab kev sib txuas lus

Peb tau txais cov ntaub ntawv xws li IP chaw nyob, browser thiab ntaus ntawv hom, kev khiav hauj lwm qhov system, app version, lus, kwv yees qhov chaw muab los ntawm IP, xa mus rau nplooj ntawv, tus cim, timestamps, tsoo cov ntaub ntawv, thiab kev ruaj ntseg thiab server cav. Peb kuj sau cov ntaub ntawv uas koj xa mus rau kev txhawb nqa lossis hauv kev tshawb fawb, kev tawm tswv yim, kev tshaj tawm kev tsim txom, thiab lwm yam kev sib txuas lus.

Cov ncuav qab zib thiab cov thev naus laus zis zoo sib xws

Peb siv cov ncuav qab zib, khaws cia hauv zos, thiab cov thev naus laus zis zoo sib xws rau kev kos npe, nyiam, kev ruaj ntseg, kev tiv thaiv kev dag, kev ua haujlwm ntawm lub platform, thiab kev ntsuas. Yog xav tau, peb thov kev tso cai ua ntej siv cov thev naus laus zis tsis tseem ceeb. Koj tus browser tuaj yeem tso cai rau koj thaiv lossis tshem tawm lawv, tab sis cov yam ntxwv tseem ceeb yuav tsum tsis ua haujlwm.

Peb tuaj yeem sib piv cov nplooj ntawv sib txawv uas siv cov ncuav qab zib sim nrog peb lub dashboard. Nws nco qab cov qauv tsim tau ntev txog 90 hnub thiab ntsuas kev mus ntsib, tsom rau lub sijhawm tshawb nrhiav, kos npe ua tiav, thiab kev ua si pib. Ib lub cim cais nco ntsoov tias qhov browser no tau kos npe rau hauv mus txog ib xyoos. Cov xwm txheej sim no tsis suav nrog koj tus email, tus lej cim nyiaj, lossis kos npe nkag.

3. Peb siv cov ntaub ntawv li cas

Peb siv cov ntaub ntawv ntiag tug rau:

  • tsim thiab ruaj ntseg nyiaj, txheeb xyuas cov neeg siv, thiab muab cov yam ntxwv ntawm Platform;
  • tswj cov tsev qiv ntawv, kev muaj cai, huab txuag, kev sib raug zoo, ntau tus neeg ua si, thiab kev txhawb nqa;
  • host, tsim, faib, pom zoo, thiab ntsuas kev ua si thiab lwm yam ntsiab lus;
  • txheej txheem kev yuav khoom, nyiaj rov qab, se, kev dag ntxias, kev tshaj tawm pov thawj, thiab them nyiaj;
  • xa kev lag luam, kev ruaj ntseg, kev cai lij choj, thiab kev pabcuam kev sib txuas lus;
  • kho tus kheej thiab txhim kho lub Platform, kuaj xyuas qhov yuam kev, thiab txhim kho cov yam ntxwv tshiab;
  • nruab nrab cov ntsiab lus, tswj peb cov ntsiab lus, tiv thaiv kev tsim txom, thiab tiv thaiv cov neeg siv thiab lub Platform; thiab
  • ua raws li txoj cai lij choj thiab tsim, siv, lossis tiv thaiv kev thov raug cai.

Nyob ntawm seb koj nyob qhov twg, peb cov hauv paus kev cai lij choj suav nrog kev ua raws li peb daim ntawv cog lus nrog koj, ua raws li txoj cai lij choj, koj qhov kev tso cai, thiab peb cov kev txaus siab raug cai hauv kev khiav hauj lwm, kev ruaj ntseg, thiab kev txhim kho Platform. Koj tuaj yeem thim qhov kev pom zoo txhua lub sijhawm uas qhov kev ua haujlwm tso siab rau kev tso cai.

4. Peb nthuav tawm cov ntaub ntawv li cas

Peb tuaj yeem nthuav tawm cov ntaub ntawv rau:

  • cov neeg muab kev pabcuam uas muab hosting, cia, authentication, analytics, kev sib txuas lus, kev txhawb nqa cov neeg siv khoom, kev ruaj ntseg, cov ntsiab lus xa, kev them nqi, se, thiab kev them nyiaj;
  • publishers thiab lwm tus neeg siv thaum xav tau los muab kev ua si, kev sib pauv, kev sib raug zoo, kev tshaj tawm, lossis lwm yam kev sib cuam tshuam koj thov;
  • cov kws tshaj lij thiab cov tub ceev xwm thaum tsim nyog tsim nyog rau kev cai lij choj, kev tswj hwm, kev tshuaj xyuas, kev ruaj ntseg, lossis kev tiv thaiv txoj cai;
  • cov neeg koom nrog kev lag luam nyob rau hauv kev sib txuas nrog nyiaj txiag, kev sib koom ua ke, kev yuav khoom, kev tsim kho dua tshiab, lossis muag cov khoom muaj nqis, raws li kev tiv thaiv tsim nyog; thiab
  • lwm tus ntawm koj qhov kev taw qhia lossis nrog koj qhov kev tso cai.

Peb tsis muag cov ntaub ntawv ntiag tug rau nyiaj lossis siv rau lwm tus neeg sab nrauv kev coj tus cwj pwm kev tshaj tawm. Cov kws kho mob ua cov ntaub ntawv raws li lawv tus kheej cov nqe lus thiab, yog tias tsim nyog, sawv cev rau peb.

5. Kev ua si, zej zog, thiab cov ntaub ntawv pej xeem

Profiles, usernames, avatars, tshuaj xyuas, cov lus qhia, kev cob qhia cov khoom, cov thawj coj nkag, kev ua tiav, nplooj ntawv kev ua si, thiab lwm cov ntsiab lus uas koj xaiv los luam tawm tuaj yeem pom rau lwm tus thiab tej zaum yuav raug txheeb xyuas lossis rov muab faib. Siv kev saib xyuas thaum tshaj tawm cov ntaub ntawv ntiag tug rau pej xeem.

Kev ua si tuaj yeem thov nkag mus rau cov kev pabcuam Platform lossis cov cuab yeej siv thiab tuaj yeem sau cov ntaub ntawv rau tus tshaj tawm. Peb xav kom cov tshaj tawm nthuav tawm lawv cov kev coj ua thiab ua raws li txoj cai siv, tab sis cov tshaj tawm muaj lub luag haujlwm ntawm cov ntaub ntawv uas lawv sau rau lawv tus kheej lub hom phiaj. Tshawb xyuas qhov kev ua si nthuav tawm thiab thov kev tso cai ua ntej ua si. Koj tuaj yeem tshaj tawm kev txhawj xeeb rau peb siv cov ntaub ntawv tiv toj hauv qab no.

6. Kev tuav pov hwm thiab kev xa mus thoob ntiaj teb

Peb khaws cov ntaub ntawv tus kheej tsuas yog ntev npaum li qhov tsim nyog rau cov hom phiaj tau piav qhia hauv tsab cai no, suav nrog thaum koj tus account tseem ua haujlwm thiab raws li xav tau rau kev ruaj ntseg, thaub qab, kev tsis sib haum xeeb, kev ua si uas twb muaj lawm, cov ntaub ntawv se thiab nyiaj txiag, kev tiv thaiv kev dag, thiab kev ua raws li txoj cai. Lub sijhawm khaws cia sib txawv raws li hom ntaub ntawv thiab cov kev xav tau. Peb tshem tawm lossis tshem tawm cov ntaub ntawv thaum nws tsis xav tau ntxiv lawm, tshwj tsis yog txoj cai tso cai lossis xav kom khaws cia.

StarHermit thiab nws cov chaw muab kev pabcuam tuaj yeem ua cov ntaub ntawv hauv Canada, Tebchaws Meskas, thiab lwm lub tebchaws. Txoj cai ntiag tug nyob ntawd yuav txawv ntawm qhov chaw koj nyob. Yog xav tau, peb siv daim ntawv cog lus thiab lwm yam kev tiv thaiv rau kev hloov mus thoob ntiaj teb.

7. Kev ruaj ntseg

Peb siv cov kev tswj hwm tsim nyog, kev tsim kho, thiab kev tiv thaiv lub cev tsim los tiv thaiv cov ntaub ntawv ntiag tug, suav nrog kev nkag mus rau kev tswj hwm thiab kev tiv thaiv tsim nyog rau cov ntaub ntawv rhiab heev. Tsis muaj qhov system muaj kev nyab xeeb kiag li, yog li tiv thaiv koj tus as khauj thiab qhia rau peb tam sim yog tias koj xav tias tsis muaj kev tso cai.

8. Koj cov kev xaiv thiab cov cai ntiag tug

Koj tuaj yeem hloov kho qee cov ntsiab lus ntawm tus account thiab kev ceev ntiag tug los ntawm lub Platform, tshem tawm cov kev pabcuam txuas, tswj xyuas qhov browser khaws cia, thiab tshem tawm ntawm kev lag luam email siv qhov txuas hauv cov lus. Koj tseem yuav tau txais kev pabcuam tsim nyog, kev ruaj ntseg, thiab kev ceeb toom kev cai lij choj.

Nyob ntawm koj qhov chaw nyob, koj tuaj yeem muaj cai nkag mus, kho, tshem tawm, lossis tau txais ib daim qauv ntawm koj cov ntaub ntawv ntiag tug; thim kev tso cai; txwv lossis txwv tsis pub ua; los yog yws rau tus tswj hwm kev ceev ntiag tug. Tej zaum koj kuj yuav muaj txoj cai rau cov ntaub ntawv hais txog kev txiav txim siab tsis siv neeg uas cuam tshuam rau koj. Tej zaum peb yuav tau txheeb xyuas koj tus kheej thiab yuav khaws cov ntaub ntawv uas tau tso cai lossis xav tau los ntawm txoj cai.

Txhawm rau thov, email info@hypedriven.com nrog rau cov ntsiab lus "StarHermit thov ceev ntiag tug." Koj kuj tuaj yeem yws mus rau Lub Chaw Haujlwm Saib Xyuas Kev Tiv Thaiv Tus Kheej ntawm Canada lossis koj tus tswj hwm hauv zos. Peb yuav tsis ntxub ntxaug koj rau kev siv txoj cai ntiag tug.

9. Lub hnub nyoog xav tau

Lub Platform yog tsim los rau cov neeg uas muaj hnub nyoog tsawg kawg yog 18 xyoo thiab tau mus txog hnub nyoog ntawm feem ntau raug cai nyob qhov twg. Peb tsis txhob txwm sau cov ntaub ntawv tus kheej los ntawm cov menyuam yaus. Yog tias koj ntseeg tias ib tug me nyuam tau muab cov ntaub ntawv rau peb, thov hu rau peb kom peb tuaj yeem tshawb xyuas thiab ua kom tsim nyog.

10. Hloov thiab tiv tauj

Peb tuaj yeem hloov kho txoj cai no raws li lub Platform lossis txoj cai hloov. Peb yuav tshaj tawm txoj cai hloov kho thiab hloov kho hnub siv. Yog tias qhov kev hloov pauv yog cov khoom siv, peb yuav muab kev ceeb toom ntxiv yog tias tsim nyog lossis xav tau.
