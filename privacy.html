1. Scope and who we are

StarHermit is operated by Hype Driven Development Inc., a British Columbia corporation ("StarHermit," "we," "us," or "our"). This policy applies to starhermit.com and the websites, applications, APIs, hosting, game distribution, commerce, community, and support services that link to it (collectively, the "Platform").

This policy does not govern a publisher's own data practices inside a game or on an external service. Those practices are covered by the publisher's privacy notice.

2. Information we collect

Account and profile information

When you sign in with Google or connect another account such as GitHub, we receive information the provider makes available, such as your name, email address, profile image, username, and provider account identifier. We do not receive your Google or GitHub password. We also collect profile details, preferences, and settings you choose to add.

Platform and game activity

We collect information needed to operate your library and the Platform, including games viewed, acquired, installed, launched, or wishlisted; playtime; cloud saves; achievements; leaderboards; multiplayer sessions; friends and presence; reviews, ratings, reports, messages, and other content; and technical events sent by games through StarHermit services.

Developer and publisher information

If you submit or manage a game, we collect repository links and metadata, builds, release and ownership records, team permissions, store materials, API usage, and communications. For monetization or verification, we may also collect legal identity, business, tax, sanctions, payment, and payout information.

Transactions

When you make a purchase or receive a payout, we collect transaction details such as the item, amount, currency, time, status, receipts, refunds, and payment-method type. Card and banking details are handled by our payment providers rather than stored directly by StarHermit. For digital-asset transactions, we may process wallet addresses and public transaction identifiers.

Device, usage, and communications

We automatically receive information such as IP address, browser and device type, operating system, app version, language, approximate location derived from IP, referral page, identifiers, timestamps, crash data, and security and server logs. We also collect information you send to support or in surveys, feedback, abuse reports, and other communications.

Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies for sign-in, preferences, security, fraud prevention, Platform operation, and measurement. Where required, we ask for consent before using non-essential technologies. Your browser may let you block or delete them, but essential features may then stop working.

3. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • create and secure accounts, authenticate users, and provide Platform features;
  • maintain libraries, entitlements, cloud saves, social features, multiplayer, and support;
  • host, build, distribute, recommend, and measure games and other content;
  • process purchases, refunds, taxes, fraud checks, publisher verification, and payouts;
  • send transactional, security, legal, and service communications;
  • personalize and improve the Platform, diagnose errors, and develop new features;
  • moderate content, enforce our terms, prevent abuse, and protect users and the Platform; and
  • comply with law and establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Depending on where you live, our legal bases include performing our contract with you, complying with legal obligations, your consent, and our legitimate interests in operating, securing, and improving the Platform. You may withdraw consent at any time where processing relies on consent.

4. How we disclose information

We may disclose information to:

  • service providers that supply hosting, storage, authentication, analytics, communications, customer support, security, content delivery, payments, tax, and payout services;
  • publishers and other users when needed to provide a game, transaction, social feature, report, or other interaction you request;
  • professional advisers and authorities when reasonably necessary for legal, regulatory, audit, security, or rights-protection purposes;
  • business transaction participants in connection with a financing, merger, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate safeguards; and
  • others at your direction or with your consent.

We do not sell personal information for money or use it for third-party cross-context behavioural advertising. Providers process information under their own terms and, where applicable, on our behalf.

5. Games, community, and public information

Profiles, usernames, avatars, reviews, guides, workshop items, leaderboard entries, achievements, game pages, and other content you choose to publish may be visible to others and may be indexed or reshared. Use care when posting personal information publicly.

Games may request access to Platform services or device features and may collect information for the publisher. We require publishers to disclose their practices and follow applicable law, but publishers are independently responsible for data they collect for their own purposes. Review the game's disclosures and permission requests before playing. You can report a concern to us using the contact information below.

6. Retention and international transfers

We keep personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including while your account is active and as needed for security, backups, disputes, existing game entitlements, tax and accounting records, fraud prevention, and legal compliance. Retention periods vary by the type of information and applicable requirements. We delete or de-identify information when it is no longer needed, unless the law permits or requires retention.

StarHermit and its providers may process information in Canada, the United States, and other countries. Privacy laws there may differ from those where you live. Where required, we use contractual and other safeguards for international transfers.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information, including access controls and protections appropriate to the information's sensitivity. No system is completely secure, so protect your account and promptly tell us if you suspect unauthorized access.

8. Your choices and privacy rights

You may update certain account details and privacy settings through the Platform, disconnect linked services, manage browser storage, and unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in the message. You will still receive necessary service, security, and legal notices.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information; withdraw consent; restrict or object to processing; or complain to a privacy regulator. You may also have a right to information about automated decisions that significantly affect you. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information where permitted or required by law.

To make a request, email info@hypedriven.com with the subject “StarHermit privacy request.” You may also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or your local regulator. We will not discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right.

9. Age requirements

The Platform is intended only for people who are at least 18 years old and have reached the age of legal majority where they live. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the Platform or law changes. We will post the revised policy and update the effective date. If a change is material, we will provide additional notice where appropriate or required.
