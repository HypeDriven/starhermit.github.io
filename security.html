Report a security issue

Email info@hypedriven.com with the subject “StarHermit security report”. Reports in English are preferred.

Include the affected URL or component, steps to reproduce the issue, its potential impact, and any supporting evidence. Do not include passwords, access tokens, or other people's personal data.

Test only with accounts and data you control. Avoid service disruption, bulk scanning, social engineering, or accessing other people's data. Stop testing if you encounter private data and report what you observed.

Please report privately and coordinate public disclosure with us while we investigate and address the issue.
